Exploring the Intersection of Rhetoric, Composition, and Artificial Intelligence

A course designed for higher education students to critically engage with AI as a rhetorical tool

Course Overview

This course examines the evolving relationship between classical rhetorical principles and modern AI technologies. Students will develop critical frameworks for understanding AI-generated text, learn to leverage AI tools ethically in their writing processes, and explore the broader implications of algorithmic rhetoric in digital spaces.

Learning Objectives

  • Analyze rhetorical strategies in both human and AI-generated texts
  • Apply rhetorical frameworks (ethos, pathos, logos) to evaluate AI-assisted writing
  • Develop ethical practices for using AI in composition and research
  • Critically examine ongoing debates about AI authorship and authenticity
  • Create effective prompts that leverage AI for rhetorical analysis

Rhetoric and AI: Current Debates

AI Authorship & Authenticity

Who is the "author" of AI-generated text? How do we understand concepts of voice, originality, and authenticity when AI systems contribute to the writing process? This debate challenges traditional notions of authorship and raises questions about citation, intellectual property, and the nature of creative work.

Algorithmic Rhetoric

AI systems make rhetorical choices through their training data, algorithms, and design. What are the implications when persuasion is automated? How do recommendation algorithms, content moderation systems, and AI writing tools embody rhetorical strategies? Understanding algorithmic rhetoric is crucial for digital literacy.

Human-AI Collaboration

Rather than viewing AI as replacement or threat, how can we conceptualize productive human-AI partnerships in composition? What does it mean to write "with" AI versus being replaced "by" AI? This debate explores new models of distributed cognition and collaborative authorship.

Ethical Considerations

What are our obligations when using AI in academic and professional writing? Questions of transparency, proper attribution, bias in AI systems, and the potential for misuse require careful ethical frameworks. How do we balance innovation with integrity?
