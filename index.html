Poetry, Feminist Rhetoric, and Artificial Intelligence

Exploring how AI interprets poetic language, gendered discourse, and feminist rhetorical traditions

Course Overview

This course centers poetry and feminist rhetorical theory in the study of artificial intelligence. Through analysis of poems by women and marginalized voices, students examine how AI systems interpret gendered language, activist poetry, and feminist rhetorical strategies. We explore the intersection of poetic craft, feminist criticism, and computational analysis to understand both the possibilities and limitations of AI in rhetorical study.

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • ✊ Analyze Maya Angelou's Rhetorical Power: Examine how Angelou builds ethos in "Still I Rise" through repetition ("I rise, I rise") and defiant voice, establishing authority as a Black woman poet resisting oppression
  • 🤖 Feminist Rhetoric & AI Interpretation: Investigate how AI systems interpret (or misinterpret) gendered language, embodied experience, and intersectional feminist discourse in poetry by women of color
  • 🎨 Poetic Devices as Rhetorical Strategies: Identify how Angelou uses metaphor ("I'm a black ocean, leaping and wide"), anaphora ("You may..."), and rhythm to create pathos and persuade readers toward social justice
  • 👩‍🎓 Agentic AI for Poetry Analysis: Develop practices where YOU analyze first (e.g., marking persuasive words in "Phenomenal Woman"), then use AI to confirm or challenge your interpretation—preserving your authorship
  • ⚖️ Intersectional Feminist Analysis: Apply Black feminist rhetorical frameworks to understand how race, gender, and power operate simultaneously in Angelou's activist poetry
  • 💬 Critical AI Prompting: Create prompts that ask AI to recognize cultural context (e.g., "How does Maya Angelou's 'Caged Bird' use the metaphor of captivity to address racial oppression?") while maintaining your critical perspective
  • 💪 Embodied & Affective Language: Assess AI's limitations in analyzing embodied experiences ("phenomenal woman, that's me") and the affective, visceral power of feminist poetry
  • 📖 Women Poets Across Traditions: Study diverse feminist voices—from Frances Harper's abolitionist poetry to contemporary spoken word—understanding how feminist rhetoric evolves across eras and movements

Rhetoric and AI: Current Debates

AI Authorship & Authenticity

Who is the "author" of AI-generated text? How do we understand concepts of voice, originality, and authenticity when AI systems contribute to the writing process? This debate challenges traditional notions of authorship and raises questions about citation, intellectual property, and the nature of creative work.

Algorithmic Rhetoric

AI systems make rhetorical choices through their training data, algorithms, and design. What are the implications when persuasion is automated? How do recommendation algorithms, content moderation systems, and AI writing tools embody rhetorical strategies? Understanding algorithmic rhetoric is crucial for digital literacy.

Human-AI Collaboration

Rather than viewing AI as replacement or threat, how can we conceptualize productive human-AI partnerships in composition? What does it mean to write "with" AI versus being replaced "by" AI? This debate explores new models of distributed cognition and collaborative authorship.

Ethical Considerations

What are our obligations when using AI in academic and professional writing? Questions of transparency, proper attribution, bias in AI systems, and the potential for misuse require careful ethical frameworks. How do we balance innovation with integrity?
