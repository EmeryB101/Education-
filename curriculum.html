Syllabus & Course Overview

Poetry, Feminist Rhetoric & AI: A Journey Through Maya Angelou's Revolutionary Voice

Human Interpretation + AI Assistance = Agentic Learning

Course Modules

📚 Module 1: Maya Angelou & Rhetorical Foundations

  • ✊ Classical rhetorical appeals in contemporary poetry
  • 🎭 Ethos, pathos, logos in "Still I Rise" and "Phenomenal Woman"
  • 💬 Prompt Exercise: "Analyze the ethos Maya Angelou builds in 'Still I Rise.' How does she establish credibility and authority as a Black woman poet?"
  • 🤖 AI Task: Compare your analysis with AI interpretation of Angelou's rhetorical strategies

🦜 Module 2: AI Prompting Through Poetry Analysis

  • ✍️ Crafting effective prompts for poetic analysis
  • 🎨 How AI interprets figurative language and metaphor
  • 💬 Prompt Exercise: "Ask AI to identify the pathos in Maya Angelou's 'Caged Bird.' Then refine your prompt to capture cultural context and racial metaphor."
  • 🤖 AI Task: Iteratively improve prompts to get deeper analysis of symbolism

👤 vs 🤖 Module 3: Human vs AI Rhetorical Interpretation

  • 💭 What humans catch that AI misses (cultural nuance, lived experience)
  • 📊 What AI catches that humans miss (patterns, frequency, structure)
  • 💬 Prompt Exercise: "Analyze the logos in Angelou's 'On the Pulse of Morning.' Then ask AI to do the same. What did each approach reveal?"
  • 🎮 Hands-On Activity: Complete the Poetry Analysis Game to practice identifying appeals

♀️ Module 4: Feminist Rhetoric & AI Ethics

  • ⚖️ How AI systems interpret (or misinterpret) feminist discourse
  • 🔍 Bias in training data and poetic analysis
  • 💬 Prompt Exercise: "Ask AI about the feminist rhetoric in Maya Angelou's work. Does it recognize intersectionality? Revise your prompt to foreground race and gender."
  • 🤖 AI Task: Test whether AI can identify specifically Black feminist rhetorical strategies

🌐 Module 5: Algorithmic Rhetoric & Digital Poetry

  • 📱 How algorithms curate and recommend poetry
  • 💻 Digital circulation of Maya Angelou's work online
  • 💬 Prompt Exercise: "Research how AI systems categorize Angelou's poetry. What tags, themes, and associations does AI make?"
  • 🤖 AI Task: Examine metadata and algorithmic framing of Black women poets

✍️ Module 6: Agentic AI & Poetic Authorship

  • 👩‍🎓 Maintaining human authorship in AI-assisted analysis
  • 🔧 AI as research assistant for poetry scholarship
  • 📝 Final Project: Write a rhetorical analysis of 3 Maya Angelou poems using agentic AI workflow (you analyze first, AI assists second)
  • 💡 Reflection: Document your process and what you learned about poetry, rhetoric, and AI

Major Assignments

Assignment 1: Comparative Rhetorical Analysis

Analyze a Maya Angelou poem using both traditional close reading and AI-assisted analysis. Write a reflection comparing the two approaches, discussing what insights each method provided and where they aligned or diverged.

Due: Week 4 | Weight: 20%

Assignment 2: Prompt Engineering Workshop

Develop a series of prompts designed to elicit effective rhetorical analysis of feminist poetry from an AI system. Test your prompts with different poems by women and document your results. Write a guide explaining your prompt design choices.

Due: Week 8 | Weight: 25%

Assignment 3: Algorithmic Rhetoric Case Study

Select a digital platform (social media, news aggregator, content recommendation system) and analyze its algorithmic rhetoric. How does the platform's design and algorithm make rhetorical choices about poetry? What values and assumptions are embedded in its operation?

Due: Week 11 | Weight: 20%

Final Project: Agentic AI Rhetorical Portfolio

Create a portfolio documenting your use of AI throughout the semester. Include rhetorical analyses of 3 Maya Angelou poems where YOU analyzed first and AI assisted second, reflections on your evolving practices, and a personal framework for ethical AI use in poetry scholarship. Present your findings to the class.

Due: Finals Week | Weight: 35%

🎮 Interactive Activity 1: Poetry Analysis Game

Like Maya Angelou rising above oppression through powerful words, YOU will rise as the primary interpreter of poetry. Mark the words that carry persuasive power—ethos (credibility), pathos (emotion), logos (logic)—in poems by revolutionary women writers. Then compare your human insight with AI's computational analysis. This is agentic AI: your voice leads, AI assists. Your interpretation matters most.

What You'll Experience

  • 🎭 Analyze Like an Activist: Identify how women poets use rhetoric to fight oppression—just as Angelou did
  • ✊ Your Voice First: YOU interpret persuasive language before AI weighs in—preserving your authorship
  • 🤖 AI as Collaborator: Compare your human understanding with AI's pattern recognition
  • 🎓 Agentic vs Generative: Learn the difference—one empowers you, one replaces you
  • 💪 Maintain Intellectual Power: AI assists your thinking; it doesn't do your thinking
  • 📖 Study Diverse Voices: Frances Harper (abolitionist), Emma Lazarus (immigrant advocate), and more feminist poets
