Course Curriculum

Structured learning path through rhetoric, composition, and AI

Course Modules

Module 1: Foundations of Rhetoric

  • Classical rhetorical theory (Aristotle, Cicero, Quintilian)
  • The rhetorical triangle: ethos, pathos, logos
  • Rhetorical situation and audience analysis
  • Stasis theory and argumentation

Module 2: Introduction to AI in Composition

  • How large language models work (basic overview)
  • Current AI writing tools: ChatGPT, Claude, and others
  • Capabilities and limitations of AI text generation
  • The concept of "prompting" as a rhetorical act

Module 3: Rhetorical Analysis with AI

  • Using AI to identify rhetorical strategies
  • Critical evaluation of AI-generated analysis
  • Comparing human vs. AI rhetorical interpretation
  • Interactive Activity: "The AI Rhetorician" Game

Module 4: Ethics and Authorship

  • Defining authorship in the age of AI
  • Academic integrity and proper attribution
  • Bias and representation in AI systems
  • Developing personal AI use policies

Module 5: Algorithmic Rhetoric

  • How algorithms persuade and influence
  • Recommendation systems as rhetorical agents
  • Content moderation and rhetorical choices
  • Platform politics and digital discourse

Module 6: Human-AI Collaboration

  • Models of productive AI partnership
  • AI as brainstorming tool, editor, and research assistant
  • Maintaining critical distance and human judgment
  • Final project: Reflective portfolio on AI-assisted writing

Sample Assignments

Assignment 1: Comparative Rhetorical Analysis

Analyze a public speech or article using both traditional close reading and AI-assisted analysis. Write a reflection comparing the two approaches, discussing what insights each method provided and where they aligned or diverged.

Assignment 2: Prompt Engineering Workshop

Develop a series of prompts designed to elicit effective rhetorical analysis from an AI system. Test your prompts with different texts and document your results. Write a guide explaining your prompt design choices.

Assignment 3: Algorithmic Rhetoric Case Study

Select a digital platform (social media, news aggregator, content recommendation system) and analyze its algorithmic rhetoric. How does the platform's design and algorithm make rhetorical choices? What values and assumptions are embedded in its operation?

Final Project: Reflective AI Portfolio

Create a portfolio documenting your use of AI throughout the semester. Include examples of AI-assisted work, reflections on your evolving practices, and a personal framework for ethical AI use in academic writing. Present your findings to the class.
